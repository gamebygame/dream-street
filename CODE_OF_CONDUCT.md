# Code of conduct

Please keep this a welcoming place to work.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Discuss the work and the evidence, never the person.
- Credit other people's ideas, art and code, and respect where things come from.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised content, or publishing anyone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and contributions that break these expectations, and may block people who repeat them. Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@L-Jovi](https://github.com/L-Jovi).

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
